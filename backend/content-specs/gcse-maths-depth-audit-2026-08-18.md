# GCSE Maths depth audit — 18 August 2026

Source: live Railway production database, queried after the lesson-time recalibration.

## Evidence

- 8 topics, 16 published lessons, and 515 published questions.
- 15 of the 16 lessons are below 250 words; 7 generated lessons are between 157 and 182 words.
- The generated lessons have three questions each. Existing lessons have five or six, except three older lessons with no linked questions.
- The only topic with one lesson is **Geometry: Measures**.
- The current study-time values are now derived from content rather than copied estimates. The 83 generated lessons were recalculated; a follow-up dry run found zero remaining changes.
- `Mastering Integers, Fractions, and Standard Form` now includes the live `fraction-lab` interactive block.

This is a depth audit, not a claim that the lessons are ready for a full Brilliant-style experience. Most lessons need richer explanation, worked examples, retrieval practice, and at least one meaningful activity before they are considered deepened.

## Definition of done for each lesson

1. Explain the idea in plain language before introducing notation.
2. Show at least three worked examples, including one deliberate misconception.
3. Give the learner something to manipulate, predict, sort, draw, or calculate—not just read.
4. Include retrieval questions that are linked to the explanation and cover easy, medium, and transfer difficulty.
5. Keep the content blocks accessible on mobile and keyboard-operable.
6. Re-run the content audit and scoring checks before publishing any replacement material.

The remaining six GCSE Maths lessons are queued after these ten. No new generated lesson was created during this audit; the goal is to deepen one coherent course rather than increase the catalogue with more thin pages.
